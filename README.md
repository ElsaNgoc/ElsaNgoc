# Hi, I'm Elsa Ngoc Dang 👋

I'm a **Business Analysis student based in Sydney, Australia**, studying a **Bachelor of Information Technology (Business Analysis major)** at **Macquarie University**.

I work where **product thinking, UX/UI design and AI-assisted building** meet. I like taking a messy problem, mapping the people and workflows behind it, designing the experience, and then **vibe coding** it into a working prototype that real users can click through.

My projects usually start with **user flows, page specs and requirements**, move into **UI design and design-to-code alignment (Figma → React)**, and end as **deployed web apps** that I iterate on quickly with AI pair-programming tools such as **Cursor**.

I'm currently growing my skills in **UX research, design systems, business & design analysis, and full-stack prototyping**, with a focus on building products that feel thoughtful, friendly and easy to use.

## 📌 Featured Projects

### ♿ [Reachable — Accessible Journey Planner](https://github.com/NhatNguyen3001/Sym-AI-Hackathon-Reachable)

An **AI-powered, step-free trip planner for Greater Sydney**, built by our team for **Sym AI Open 2026** at Macquarie University. It plans the whole trip, including the walk or roll at each end, checks live station lifts, and explains slopes in plain words such as "Easy slope" or "A bit tiring".

My focus was the **UX/UI of the web app**: a **map-first navigation** with hazard pins, trip choices placed directly on the map, a mobile-friendly layout with corner controls and a hidden route key, colour-coded train lines with dashed walks and solid rides, and **step-by-step spoken guidance** once a trip starts.

`Accessibility` `Inclusive Design` `Map UX` `Mobile UX` `Voice Guidance` `Next.js` `Hackathon`

🔗 [Live showcase](https://reachable-five.vercel.app)

### 🎓 [Sinaptik Mentor Portal — UX Prototype](https://github.com/ElsaNgoc/sinaptik_prototype)

A **mentor (instructor) portal prototype** for the EdTech company Sinaptik, designed around the problem of *fragmented collaboration* between learners, mentors and AI grading.

I mapped the **end-to-end user flows**, wrote the **page specification and Figma alignment review**, defined the **dashboard KPIs**, and built most of the prototype: a triage dashboard, task calendar, learner profiles, AI-assisted marking, inbox and chat, with **English / Bahasa Indonesia** switching and a **mobile UI adaptation plan**.

`UX Design` `User Flows` `Figma → Code` `Dashboard Design` `i18n` `React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS`

🔗 [Live prototype](https://sinaptik-prototype.vercel.app)

### 📚 [ComicWeb — Comic Reading Platform & CMS](https://github.com/ElsaNgoc/ComicWeb)

A **comic / manga reading website** with a full **admin CMS**, designed around a soft **watercolour visual identity**.

Admins can manage series, chapters, tags and page images, and customise the site's look (banner, sidebar content, colour palette, layout and a **Vietnamese-safe Google Fonts picker**) without touching code. Readers get a clean chapter reader, and selected chapters can be **unlocked with a keyword** linked to an affiliate campaign.

`UI Theming` `Typography` `Content Design` `CMS Design` `Next.js` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Cloudflare R2`

🔗 [Live site](https://comic-web-jet.vercel.app)

### 💌 [Our Memory — Private Web App for Couples](https://github.com/NgocElsa05/our_memory_web_full_version)

A **private, pastel-themed web app for two people** to keep shared memories: photo gallery with calendar and album views, love letters, a shared YouTube playlist, and a "discovery" moodboard for getting to know each other.

Designed **mobile-first** (bottom navigation on phones, sidebar on desktop), installable as a **PWA**, and extended with an **iOS home-screen widget**.

`Emotional Design` `Mobile-First UX` `PWA` `React` `Supabase` `Cloudinary` `Capacitor`

🔗 [Live app](https://our-memory-web-full-version.vercel.app)

### 🔮 [Tử Vi Lá Số — AI Birth Chart Reader](https://github.com/NgocElsa05/tuvilaso)

A web app that turns a **Tử Vi (Vietnamese Purple Star astrology) birth chart** into a personalised four-chapter report, written by an AI that may only reason from a classic reference book.

I designed the **content and user journey**: an **age-aware context survey** before the reading, advice rewritten for four life stages, a report with a jump-to table of contents, a daily "what to do today" page, and **guest-friendly privacy** where nothing is kept unless the user signs in. Built through **vibe coding**, with a Vietnamese interface for Vietnamese users.

`Content Design` `Conversational UX` `Prompt Design` `Privacy by Design` `Python` `OpenAI / Gemini` `Turso`

### 📈 [Algothon 2026 — Trading Strategy](https://github.com/ElsaNgoc/algothon26-starter-code)

Our team's entry for the **Susquehanna x UNSW FinTech Society Algothon 2026**, an algorithmic trading hackathon.

I developed the strategy through **AI-assisted iteration**, documenting every experiment in an **AI change log**. The final strategy combines a cross-sectional ridge regression ensemble with an overlay for one special instrument, and I built a **walk-forward validation gate** to stop us shipping strategies that only looked good on the public data.

`Analytical Thinking` `Experiment Tracking` `Vibe Coding` `Python` `NumPy` `pandas`
